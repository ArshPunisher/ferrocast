# Vision

## One line

Ferrocast is the free, open-source, local-first companion app for streamers: alerts, overlays, chat, chatbot and moderation for Twitch, YouTube and Kick, on macOS, Windows, Linux and the web.

## Who it is for

- **Small and mid-size gaming streamers** on one PC who feel every lost frame and can't afford to babysit five tools.
- **Multistreamers** who stream to Twitch, YouTube and Kick at once and want one chat, one alert box and one ban button.
- **Mac and Linux streamers** who are second-class in today's tools.
- **People leaving cloud tools** that shut down, got sold or started charging, and who want to own their setup.

## Promises

1. **It works without us.** The desktop app runs fully on the user's computer. If our servers vanish, alerts, chat, the bot and moderation keep working for Twitch and YouTube.
2. **You can take everything with you.** One click exports overlays with their media, commands, points, viewers and tip history in open formats.
3. **Free stays free.** Nothing that is free today will move behind a paywall later.
4. **Few clicks.** First run is: sign in, pick a look, "Add to OBS". Any common task is at most three clicks from the home screen.
5. **Light while gaming.** The engine is Rust; the window closes to the tray; overlays pause when OBS hides them.
6. **We never hold your money.** Tips go straight from viewers to the streamer's own payment account.
7. **We never talk to your audience.** No upsells in chat, no branding on overlays.

## What we build (v1)

- Alert box with variations, conditions, templates, sounds, custom CSS/JS, test buttons and a "why didn't this fire?" log.
- Chat box, event list, goals, labels, combined viewer count.
- Merged chat for Twitch, YouTube and Kick, with reply-to-source, as a window and as an OBS dock.
- Chatbot: commands, variables, timers, counters, quotes, spam filters.
- Cross-platform moderation: one ban list, ban everywhere, hate-raid panic mode, bot and scam defence.
- TTS with an approval queue (off by default).
- Tip page with Razorpay (UPI), Stripe, PayPal and Ko-fi, where the streamer's legal name is never shown, and alerts only on settled payments.
- OBS control and one-click "Add to OBS".
- Importers from StreamElements and Streamlabs; export everything.

## What we don't build

Broadcasting or encoding, restreaming, video calls, merch, sponsorship marketplaces, TikTok (no official API).

## How we make money

The desktop app is free and GPL-3.0. **Ferrocast Pro** sells things that cost us money to run: a 24/7 cloud bot, sync and the web dashboard, smart filters, premium voices and themes, and a custom tip page domain. Pro is paid through Razorpay and priced for India and emerging markets first.
